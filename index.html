    About this passport

    How it works

    1. Pick a stop on the map or in the list, and go there.
    2. Check in when you arrive. That stamps your passport.
    3. Answer the stop's trivia question. Miss it and a fresh one comes up, so nobody gets stuck.
    4. Badges arrive as the stamps and right answers add up.

    Your privacy

    Your location is read only at the moment you tap Check in or Find me. The app records that you checked in and when. It does not keep your position or send it anywhere.

    Your name, stamps, answers and badges are saved in this browser, on this phone only. They do not follow you to another phone, and clearing your browser data erases them. The Directions button opens Google Maps, which has its own terms. The typefaces are loaded from Google and, where the street map shows, its pictures are loaded from OpenStreetMap, the way any web page loads fonts and pictures.
